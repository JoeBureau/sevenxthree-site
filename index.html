sevenXthree

Built so other people can understand you.

SevenXThree trains speech recognition on one person's own voice — their real recordings, not a generic model — and puts a communication board underneath it for the days it is not enough.

What it is

A model for one voice

Recordings a caregiver approves train a model on the primary user's own speech. It retrains as their speech changes, so it keeps up with a growing child or a progressive condition.

A board that stays underneath

Tap a phrase or say your own. The board works on every device and is there on the days recognition is unsure — a communication tool whose only input path can fail is not one a family can rely on.

Practice that does double duty

Scenarios, games and conversation are how the recordings accumulate. Practising and building the model are one activity, not two.

Everyone who helps, on one board

Invite family, therapists and teachers. The board and its settings stay in sync across their devices.

How it works

  1. Record The primary user speaks; whoever supports them reviews the audio and approves the transcript in Studio, on the web.
  2. Train The first model builds once there are enough approved recordings, and it gets better every week you record.
  3. Be understood Speech Companion types what they say into any app on a phone or tablet, and the Chrome extension does the same beside any text box on the web.

What you get

Speech Companion

iPhone, iPad and Android. The board, the keyboard and the recognition, wherever they are.

Studio

The workbench, on the web. Review recordings, run practice, and watch progress over months rather than sessions.

The Chrome extension

The same recognition and the same board, next to any text box on any website.
